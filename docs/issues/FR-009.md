# [FR-009] Background Music (BGM) & Ambient Sound Effects

## Labels
`Future`, `enhancement`, `bgm-layering`, `audio-dsp`

## User Story
As an audio designer, I want to add subtle background music and atmospheric sound effects under narration tracks with automatic audio ducking.

## Proposed Solution

### Overview
Add a secondary timeline track for Background Music (BGM) in the studio editor. Apply sidechain audio ducking (lowering music volume by 12-18dB whenever speech is active).

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Import MP3/WAV music tracks.
- [ ] Adjustable ducking ratio and music volume slider.
- [ ] Loop BGM seamlessly across chapters.
